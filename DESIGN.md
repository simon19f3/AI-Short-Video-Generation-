# Design

**Audience** — Family and students just starting university.

**Intent** — Show how curiosity and family support helped an uncertain freshman grow into a graduate.

The opening baby hand and the family member's hand in the last shot connect the beginning and the celebration. Each shot has a stated purpose in the storyboard. The freshman arrives at the actual Addis Ababa University gate and hesitates. Classroom discussion and peer study show how curiosity became lasting friendship. The father tutoring scenes reuse the same supporting character design.
